Calculation method & assumptions

The rate entered is an assumption applied consistently across the modelled TDs and reinvestments. The estimate uses a simplified quarterly-compounding approximation. Each contribution is modelled as a separate one-year TD; the rate on an actual TD is governed by the rate and rules applicable when that deposit is opened. Future reinvestment rates may change. Contributions not yet matured by the selected horizon are shown at principal value. Taxes/TDS, actual product-specific rounding, rate changes and premature closure are not modelled. Profit percentage is cumulative estimated profit divided by total contributions, not an annualized return/XIRR.

Questions, answered

Frequently asked questions

Understand the savings approach, interest assumptions and important Time Deposit conditions.

What is Sanchoyer Sopno?

It is an illustrative savings-planning tool that models optional monthly contributions into separate one-year Post Office Time Deposits and shows an estimated future corpus. It is a planning approach, not a separate government savings scheme or recurring deposit account.

Can I stop contributing and resume any time?

Yes. This is a flexible planning approach, not a contractual monthly contribution plan. You can stop making new deposits and resume later according to your circumstances. Each Time Deposit you have already opened remains a separate account and continues under its own terms until maturity or permitted closure. Stopping future contributions does not itself close existing deposits.

Are returns guaranteed? Is this market-linked like a mutual fund SIP?

Post Office TD interest is not linked to stock-market performance. For each actual TD, the applicable government-notified rate at the time of opening applies according to the scheme rules for that deposit's tenure. However, this calculator's final corpus is only an illustration: future TD rates on reinvestment may change, and actual maturity values depend on booking dates, scheme rules, rounding and tax treatment. Mutual fund SIP returns are market-linked and are not guaranteed.

What happens if I close a Time Deposit prematurely?

Post Office TD premature closure is subject to scheme rules and is generally not permitted before six months. If closed after six months but before one year, interest is payable at the applicable Post Office Savings Account rate for completed months. Premature closure of longer-tenure TDs after one year is subject to reduced-interest rules and adjustment of interest already paid. Conditions differ by tenure; confirm the current rules and amount payable with the post office before closing.

What happens when a Time Deposit matures?

The maturity amount is deposited automatically in your POSB account.

What are the requirement of opening a Time Deposit?

For opening a Time Deposit account, KYC and two copies photos is required along with existing POSB account for crediting the TD maturity amount.

How do the three plans work?

Plan A models new contributions during the first 12 months, then no further contributions while eligible proceeds may be reinvested. Plan B models a fixed monthly contribution for the full selected horizon. Plan C models monthly contributions that increase by the selected percentage each year throughout the selected horizon.

What are PLI and RPLI?

Your savings help you plan for tomorrow. Life cover can help protect the people who depend on you. Postal Life Insurance (PLI) is available to eligible categories such as government and public-sector employees and qualifying professionals or graduates. Rural Postal Life Insurance (RPLI) extends life-insurance options to eligible rural residents. Postal staff can explain the available plans, premium, sum assured and policy terms so you can consider cover suited to your needs. Eligibility and acceptance are subject to applicable rules and policy conditions; a Time Deposit does not include life insurance. Explore PLI · Explore RPLI.
